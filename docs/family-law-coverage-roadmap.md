# Indian family-law coverage and release roadmap

Baseline audited on 28 November 2026 at `2488d84`. The local API is an evidence-based intake that offers potential routes, clarification and handoff. Coverage here means a reachable path from the user’s evidence through legal predicates and rendered output. The official sources below establish the scope of work. Implementation and independent legal review have separate acceptance checks.

## Executable coverage

Graph v4.1 declares 36 records, 68 nodes and 104 edges. Sixteen records are procedure or document guidance. The engine traverses the graph for structural and choice filtering, then runs the Python Stage 2 predicates for legal dispositions. A reachable leaf by itself does not establish that the corresponding remedy can be selected, cited or rendered.

- **16 statutory or Act-level IDs can be emitted by baseline Stage 2.** HMA §§9, 13, 13B, 24–27, BNSS §144, PWDVA §§17–20 and 22, BNS §316, the Guardians and Wards Act and the Registration of Births and Deaths Act. The last two use Act-level IDs. The legacy profile adds HMGA §6 and the child-welfare principle as custody companions. [Stage 2 implementation](../src/laya_v7_graph.py) · [Graph interpreter](../src/laya_graph_engine.py)
- **Two more routes are live in the narrow HTTP maintenance intake.** BNSS §144(3) for recovery and §146 for variation require a grounded qualifying order and an identified action. Stage 2 uses conditional dispositions and the HTTP adapter can take over on the existing-order path. Missing, older or mixed order cases may go to clarification or review. [Chat API](../src/laya_chat_api.py) · [BNSS §§144–147 and 531](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/20340/1/bnss%2C_2023.pdf)
- **HMA judicial separation was missing at baseline.** The extractor could suppress divorce when it recognized the distinction, but it had no §10 outcome. The release slice should require an affirmative request for a separation decree, confirmation of the governing Act and conditional language around statutory grounds. Keep §13A alternative relief in a divorce proceeding separate. [Hindu Marriage Act §§2, 10, 13, 13A and 19](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/5399/1/a1955-25.pdf)

## 1. Correct the assumptions with the highest user impact

- **Governing Act and forum.** Collect the Act shown in the marriage record or court papers, identify the pending proceeding or relief and ask for territorially relevant facts. HMA §19 and SMA §31 set filing criteria. Where a Family Court has been established, Family Courts Act §§3, 7 and 8 affect where covered matters are heard. Keep a typed unknown state for a missing record, conflicting dates, an unconfirmed governing law or a location that has not been checked. An explicit SMA statement should never lead to an HMA confirmation prompt. [HMA](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/5399/1/a1955-25.pdf) · [SMA](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/20574/1/special_marriage_act.pdf) · [Family Courts Act](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/19489/1/the_family_court_act%2C1984.pdf)
- **Separate safety, domestic-violence relief and ordinary maintenance.** An independent baseline probe that expressly denied domestic violence returned an urgent status and PWDVA §18. A child-expense question that expressly denied shared living and marriage returned PWDVA §20 alongside BNSS §144. Add paired tests for denied, historical, alleged and likely harm, economic abuse, former domestic relationships, and third-party reporting. Resolve the actor, relationship, qualifying harm and current safety risk separately. Keep urgent help available independently of any established legal route. Check the facts needed for §20 and the distinction between an original §12 application, §26 relief in other proceedings and §27 jurisdiction. [PWDVA §§2, 3, 12, 18, 20, 26 and 27](https://upload.indiacode.nic.in/showfile?actid=AC_CEN_13_14_00008_200543_1517807325788&filename=a2005-43.pdf)
  - The scoped chatbot fix covers these explicit patterns and preserves distinct affirmative harm and safety cues. Treat the rule as limited routing evidence. Former shared households and possible rights to reside require their own analysis. [Prabha Tyagi v Kamlesh Devi, Supreme Court, paragraphs 58–62](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2019/45645/45645_2019_11_1502_35736_Judgement_12-May-2022.pdf)
- **Effective dates and overlapping orders.** Keep alleged conduct date, filing date, pendency at commencement, issuing statute and issuing forum as separate fields. Test cases on both sides of 1 July 2024 and across the transition. BNSS §531 and BNS §358 have different savings provisions. Capture existing maintenance applications and awards so a new route does not imply automatic double recovery. Treat missing dates, contradictory paperwork and unverified legacy order procedures as clarification or legal review. [BNSS §§144–147 and 531](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/20340/1/bnss%2C_2023.pdf) · [BNSS commencement notification](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2024/252354.pdf) · [BNS §§316 and 358](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/20062/1/a2023-45.pdf) · [BNS commencement notification](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2024/252353.pdf) · [Rajnesh v Neha, Supreme Court](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2018/37875/37875_2018_39_1501_24602_Judgement_04-Nov-2020.pdf)

**Acceptance.** A negative, uncertain or contradicted prerequisite cannot produce a positive legal disposition. Genuine urgent scenarios still receive safety guidance. Specific forums require sufficient location and proceeding evidence. Any new route must distinguish its sources and consequences from remedies that can apply in parallel.

## 2. Expand by verified route rather than by adding Act names

- **Matrimonial relief.** Implement a separately tested SMA path for restitution and separation (§§22–23), nullity (§§24–25), divorce (§§27–28) and forum (§31). Stage the distinct §§36–38 maintenance and custody predicates separately. Add the missing HMA nullity and alternate-relief branches with their own conditions. One ceremony, a name or a state of residence should never establish the marriage regime by itself. [SMA](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/20574/1/special_marriage_act.pdf) · [HMA](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/5399/1/a1955-25.pdf)
- **Children and financial support.** Distinguish guardianship of person or property, territorial jurisdiction, interim protection and welfare under GWA §§7, 9, 12 and 17. Gate HMGA §§6 and 13 on that Act’s applicability. Add PWDVA §§21 and 23 for temporary custody and interim or ex parte relief when the facts and posture support them. Evaluate HAMA §§18–22 and the separate parents and senior citizens maintenance route, including the latter Act’s §12 election. [GWA](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/2318/1/189008.pdf) · [HMGA](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/1649/1/195632.pdf) · [PWDVA](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/15436/1/protection_of_women_from_domestic_violence_act%2C_2005.pdf) · [HAMA](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/1638/1/AA1956____78.pdf) · [Parents and Senior Citizens Act](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/2033/1/200756.pdf)
- **Marriage-regime gaps.** Establish separate reviewed paths for dissolution and post-divorce rights under the Muslim statutes, matrimonial proceedings under the Divorce Act and the Parsi Act, and cross-border matters under the Foreign Marriage Act. Include the relevant state legislation and amendments as separate jurisdictional inputs. The initial release can identify the governing-law gap and offer a handoff without applying HMA by analogy. [Dissolution of Muslim Marriages Act](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/indiacode/handle/123456789/2404?view_type=browse) · [Muslim Women (Protection of Rights on Divorce) Act](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/1873/1/198625.pdf) · [Muslim Women (Protection of Rights on Marriage) Act](https://indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/11564/1/a2019-20.pdf) · [Mohd Abdul Samad v State of Telangana, Supreme Court](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2024/3533/3533_2024_11_1501_53688_Judgement_10-Jul-2024.pdf) · [Divorce Act](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/2280/1/A1869-04.pdf) · [Parsi Marriage and Divorce Act](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/2476/1/a1936_____3.pdf) · [Foreign Marriage Act](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/1720/5/A1969-33.pdf) · [Uttarakhand UCC government orders](https://www.ucc.uk.gov.in/government-orders)
- **Adoption, succession and child marriage.** Adoption and succession need separate pathways from custody and matrimonial property. Build the HAMA adoption and Juvenile Justice Act adoption paths separately, with amended §61 and current CARA rules for the latter. Succession depends on the governing law and facts including the date of death, the will, relationship and property type. Child marriage needs its own safety, age, timing, voidability and forum checks. Replace the broad birth registration identifier with distinct delayed-registration and correction paths after validating state procedures. [HAMA](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/1638/1/AA1956____78.pdf) · [Amended JJ Act](https://upload.indiacode.nic.in/showfile?actid=AC_CEN_13_14_000010_201602_1517807328168&filename=a2016-2.pdf&type=actfile) · [CARA adoption regulations](https://cara.wcd.gov.in/PDF/adoption_regulations_2022_2709.pdf) · [Hindu Succession Act](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/indiacode/handle/123456789/1713?view_type=browse) · [Prohibition of Child Marriage Act](https://upload.indiacode.nic.in/showfile?actid=AC_JH_70_814_00001_00001_1547115629125&filename=a2007-06.pdf&type=actfile) · [Registration of Births and Deaths Act §§13 and 15](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/1682/1/A1969-18_english.pdf)

**Acceptance.** For each added route, record the exact provision, official source, effective period, jurisdiction, factual prerequisites, exclusions, forum rule and its review status. Verify operative text and amendments against the Gazette and relevant court orders before marking a route eligible for release. Include cases for an unknown Act, the closest other Act, a false premise, an existing order and a corrected answer. Show unknown scope as a separate outcome from an ineligible claim. [NALSA legal aid](https://nalsa.gov.in/legal-aid/)

## 3. Measure generalization and legal correctness separately

- **Frozen synthetic validation measures familiar scenario structure.** The repository's split audit found that 500/500 validation items share the same block signature with train, and 500/500 have a different-language training sibling on a block-plus-label proxy. It also reported a shared training word 8-gram for 489/500. The signature is a family-level proxy. Exact translation pairing requires scenario identifiers. Group new splits by scenario family and tag translation and paraphrase siblings together. Fit thresholds on the development partition. Leave the existing frozen files as versioned regression fixtures. [Split audit](impl_phaseA_A5_split_audit.md)
- **Kaanoon measures agreement with the current router on public question wording.** Its 28 expected provision sets come from Stage 2, and seven are empty under that graph. The dataset notes explicitly record judicial separation as a missing route. Keep those regression labels frozen and test any new remedy on separately authored examples. The reported 8-gram check after deduplication does not establish absence of semantic overlap, independent legal correctness or absence from model pretraining. [Kaanoon dataset notes](../data/kaanoon_eval/README.md)
- **Give the gold-fact graph and the classifier separate scores.** Start with an independently reviewed development set organized by statute, remedy, claimant, procedural posture, date boundary, jurisdiction, language and negation/uncertainty. Keep all translations, paraphrases, source-case variants and multi-turn continuations in the same group. Collect examples from written statutes and synthetic facts without using the graph output as the annotation answer. Use the existing four-way graph oracle on independent labels, then score the whole HTTP conversation for clarifications, corrections, citations and refusals. A graph error with correct inputs identifies a rule gap. Extractor errors with a passing graph identify evidence or data gaps. [Graph oracle](../scripts/run_graph_oracle.py) · [HTTP benchmark](../tests/chat_backtest.py)
- **Publish release claims per verified slice.** Report exact sections with sample sizes, per-language and per-issue coverage, withholding, positive and negative safety errors, wrong governing law, wrong forum and unsupported citation. Keep independent blind cases separate from prompt and threshold tuning. Require zero materially unsafe positives and misses in the reviewed release set, verify supported route sources and repeat the end-to-end check before broadening coverage. A 10/10 score is a measured result for a version, scope and benchmark.
